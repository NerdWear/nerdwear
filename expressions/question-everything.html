The story behind the Expression

Questioning is not the same as rejecting

Healthy skepticism asks what the evidence is, how the claim was tested, what assumptions are hidden, whether another explanation fits, and what would change our minds. Reflexive disbelief is not skepticism; it is just another form of certainty. Scientific questioning is productive because it creates a path toward a better answer.

Feynman and the role of doubt

In talks later collected in The Meaning of It All, Feynman emphasized uncertainty as a necessary part of scientific thinking. In the Feynman Lectures he similarly described experiment as the judge of scientific knowledge and reminded students that what we know remains open to correction. The spirit is not cynicism. It is disciplined humility.

The integrity test

Questioning should point inward as readily as outward. Are we favoring evidence because it supports the answer we wanted? Did we report the inconvenient result? Are we confusing confidence with proof? Feynman’s famous discussions of scientific integrity emphasized exactly that danger: the easiest person to fool can be yourself.

Why wear it?

Because “Question Everything” can be a reminder rather than a slogan aimed at other people. Question the claim. Question the model. Question the default. Question your own favorite explanation. Then do the harder thing: follow the evidence when it answers back.
