Smart can look good.

NerdWear started with a simple idea: intelligent design does not have to look like conference swag. A formula can be elegant. A coding phrase can have style. A science reference can work on a vintage cap, a sharp snapback or a clean beanie without screaming for attention.

We are building pieces for people who notice details, care about the idea behind the design and would rather wear something with a story than another random logo.

Real people. Real styles.

Built to be worn, not just understood.

The idea matters. So does the fit, the texture, the color and the way the piece actually looks on a person.

Equations, ideas, code and just enough attitude.

For math, science and technology people

Embroidered math hats and science caps with an actual point of view.

NerdWear is building a collection of embroidered math hats, physics hats, computer science caps, coding hats, STEM beanies, equation hats and geek-inspired accessories for students, engineers, programmers, scientists, teachers, makers and lifelong learners. The designs range from famous equations and mathematical constants to coding culture, scientific ideas and understated statements about curiosity.

Designed for people who notice

Geek gear without the souvenir-shop feeling.

Instead of treating the formula as a novelty graphic, NerdWear treats the idea as the design. Embroidery, hat shape, thread color, wash, structure and contrast all matter. The goal is simple: create intelligent apparel and accessories that look good even before someone understands the reference, and get even better once they do.
