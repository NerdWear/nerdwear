Under development

More NerdWear is on the way.

Hats are the right place to begin. They are not the end of the idea. The next Expressions are already taking shape on products you can carry, use, and keep on the desk next to whatever problem you are supposed to be solving.

Next up: drinkware

Same ideas. New surfaces.

Drinkware is the next major category we are developing. These previews show the direction: equations, constants, and Expressions that can wrap, pair, or use two sides of an object in ways a hat cannot.

Preview images are directional. Final products, colors, sizes, and availability may change before release.

On the drawing board

There are more places for an Expression to live.

Possible future directions include shirts, hoodies, stickers, desk and office items, additional accessories, and other useful products that fit naturally with the NerdWear brand. These are directions, not release-date promises.
